Concerts

The amazing music majors, musician faculty, and other incredibly talented guests play frequently at BYU-I. Experience music in a way that your phone or speaker never can.

Dancing

Come show off your skills, or gain some new ones, by participating in the various dancing events on campus. There is country, Latin, and ballroom dancing at 220A Grand Ballroom Section A. Latin is on Tuesday 8:00pm – 11:00pm, Country on Wednesday 8:00pm – 11:00pm, and Ballroom on Thursday 7:00pm – 10:00pm. Be sure to check that week's calendar before you go!

Crazy Fun

Don't forget that there are so many other things happening on campus. There is laser tag, roller skating, game night, and Sports Groups to join and play. Check the campus calendar for this week's schedule!

Crossroads

If at any point throughout the week you are feeling hungry, visit the Crossroads. This is a great place to bump into old or new friends and grab a bite to eat.
