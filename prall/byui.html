Beyond the classroom

Find your kind of fun

Try something new or bring a few friends along.

Concerts

Students, faculty, and special guests perform regularly. Experience live music in a way your phone or speaker can't match. View music events.

Dancing

Country, Latin, and ballroom dances are great ways to meet people and learn something new. Check the campus calendar for current days, times, and locations.

Games & sports

Look out for game nights, laser tag, roller skating, and activities with friends. You can also find intramural sports.

Need a quick break?

Stop by the Crossroads

If you're hungry during the week, the Crossroads is a great place to get a bite to eat and catch up with friends.
