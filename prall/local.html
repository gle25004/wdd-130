Local Events

Rexburg is an amazing city with so many great places to visit. If you have a vehicle, there are also many places close by that you could visit, including the Tetons, West Yellowstone, the St. Anthony Sand Dunes, and more. Get the knack for exploring by checking out some of the following locations.

Parks

  • Smith Park
  • Porter Park (hosts local events)
  • Nature Park
  • Eagle Park & Campground

Local Sites

  • Legacy Flight Museum
  • Museum of Rexburg
  • Gravity Factory
  • Roots and Boots
  • FatCats (theater and bowling)
  • Paramount 5 (theater)
  • Romance Theater

Library

Join in on plenty of programs and events happening at the local library. Check out a book as well by getting a local library card for locals and students. Remember to bring an official legal document with your local address and identification when you go.

Shops

  • Holidae Fun & Games (play board games for free!)
  • Ross Dress for Less
  • Deseret Industries
  • Deseret Book
  • Mike's Music

Restaurants/Food

  • Jimmy John's
  • Tropical Smoothie Café
  • Kainoa's
  • Mo' Bettahs Hawaiian Style Food
  • Righteous Slice (pizza)
  • Café Rio
  • Costa Vida
  • Teton Bagel
  • And More!

Visit Idaho Falls

In the end, if nothing here strikes your fancy, Idaho Falls is only a short hop away. While we will miss you here in Rexburg, always have fun wherever you go.
