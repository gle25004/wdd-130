Things to see & do

Rexburg is full of places to explore. If you have a car, the St. Anthony Sand Dunes, Tetons, and Yellowstone area make for even more adventures.

Parks

  • Smith Park
  • Porter Park (local events)
  • Nature Park
  • Eagle Park & Campground

Places to visit

  • Legacy Flight Museum
  • Museum of Rexburg
  • Gravity Factory
  • Roots and Boots
  • FatCats (movies & bowling)
  • Paramount 5
  • Romance Theater

Local library

Madison Library has books, activities, and community programs. If you live nearby, check what you need to get a local library card.
