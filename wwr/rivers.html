WHAT SOUNDS GOOD TO YOU?

THREE WAYS TO GET OUTSIDE.

From relaxing by the river to taking on rougher water, here are a few things that make rafting trips memorable.

01 / THE RIVER

SPEND A DAY ON THE WATER

A river trip is a great way to enjoy the outdoors and see places that you might not get to visit on foot. Different stretches of water can offer completely different experiences.

  • Enjoy the scenery along the river
  • Bring friends or family for the day
  • Choose conditions that match your experience
Have a question? Contact us →

02 / CAMPING

STAY A LITTLE LONGER

Some of the best parts of being outside happen after the rafting is done. Camping gives you more time to explore, relax, and hang out around the fire.

  • Plan for changing weather
  • Pack water, food, and camp essentials
  • Leave your campsite cleaner than you found it
Questions about camping? →

03 / WHITEWATER

TAKE ON THE RAPIDS

Whitewater adds a different level of excitement. Rapids can be unpredictable, so planning ahead and following experienced guides and local safety advice really matters.

  • Wear a properly fitted life jacket
  • Learn about river conditions before going
  • Choose appropriate routes and guides
