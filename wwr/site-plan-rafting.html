01

THE FOUNDATION

THE IDEA

PURPOSE

FIND YOUR ADVENTURE.

The purpose of Dry Oar is to help people discover exciting whitewater rafting adventures while providing useful information about rivers, camping, and outdoor experiences.

The website will give visitors an easy way to learn about rafting and find an adventure that fits their interests.

AUDIENCE

PEOPLE WHO LOVE OUTDOORS.

The target audience is people who enjoy outdoor activities, adventure, rafting, camping, and spending time in nature.

Visitors may be individuals, friends, couples, or families looking for a fun outdoor experience. The website should work well on both computers and mobile devices.

TYPOGRAPHY

HEADINGS

CHASE THE RAPIDS

Bebas Neue is used for large headings. Its bold appearance gives the website an energetic outdoor personality.

BODY TEXT

Explore rivers, discover new places, and experience the outdoors with friends and family.

Montserrat is used for normal text because it is clean and easy to read.
